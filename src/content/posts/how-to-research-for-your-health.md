<section class="research-blog">
  <h2>Researching Your Own Health Solutions</h2>
  <p>
    We are not allowed to tell you all the things our products do without being accused of making claims.
    We use FDA-compliant language that talks about what systems the products support.
    We recommend that you learn how to do solid research—for yourself and your family!
    Here are some ways you can do it:
  </p>

  <ol>
    <li>
      Go to
      <a href="https://pubmed.gov" target="_blank" class="highlight-link">PubMed.gov</a>
      and search the name of the product or ingredient you’re looking into. Try combining that ingredient
      with terms like “cancer” or “diabetes.”
    </li>
    <li>
      Try searching the same terms on
      <a href="https://www.perplexity.ai" target="_blank" class="highlight-link">Perplexity.ai</a> —
      a search engine designed around research.
    </li>
    <li>
      Go to
      <a href="https://aidshivawareness.org" target="_blank" class="highlight-link">Aidshivawareness.org</a>
      and click on their “Research” tab to see research on a lot of products from this website.
      They have done a lot of research on the flax hull lignans, AfterShot Zeolite, and Essiac Tea in particular.
    </li>
  </ol>
</section>
